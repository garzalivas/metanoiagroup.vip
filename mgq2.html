I

One house, many works

What El Atelier MX is, and what it makes possible.

El Atelier MX is the production house and publishing imprint behind the work of Mario Garza Livas. It produces his video podcasts, publishes his books and manages the brands from which he gives keynote talks. It is not one more voice: it is the house that lets a voice travel far without wearing out along the way.

The way it works fits in a single idea: share everything that has no identity, and never what does. The team, the processes, the editing and the archive are common. The name, the promise, the language and the audience of each work are looked after separately. That is why lived faith in the real world and late-night satire, geopolitics and Mexican football, markets and unsolved mysteries can live side by side without blurring into one another.

Its motto says it all: stories that matter. Not content to fill a calendar, but work made to last.

The house is one. The works are many.

II

The video podcasts of the house

Twenty-three channels, one voice. The subject changes; the lens remains.

This is the heart of El Atelier MX. Twenty-three video podcasts that range from faith lived in the real world to geopolitics, from family business succession to unsolved mysteries, from investigating Mexican football to rankings and satire. They are not twenty-three separate projects: they are one way of seeing, applied to twenty-three territories.

They are organized in seven editorial houses. Six have a territory of their own, explored in depth. The seventh, El Profesor Mario Presenta, is also home to sixteen shows and gathers on a single channel everything the others publish: enter through a show and you see one territory; enter the house and you see the whole body of work.

Hosted by Mario Garza Livas

All twenty-three channels share the same host and the same way of seeing. Register, pace and format change —from sober analysis to narration, from rankings to satire— but the person speaking is always the same.
