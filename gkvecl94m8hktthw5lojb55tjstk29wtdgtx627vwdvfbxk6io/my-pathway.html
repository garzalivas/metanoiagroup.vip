I

The offering

None of these goals stands on its own. All of them are handed over first.

Act of surrender

“I place these goals in the hands of Christ, willing to live only what He wants for me, abandoning myself with confidence to His providence and His perfect will.”

This is not a career plan or a wish list. It is the order of the things I want my life to produce, written down so that it can be reviewed, corrected and — when necessary — let go of. Thirteen goals across five horizons, from what is burning today to what must still be standing long after me.

Each goal is named from the place I occupy while pursuing it: free man, son of God, family man, pilgrim, citizen. These are not professional titles. They are the concrete forms one single vocation takes across a lifetime.
