I

The north

A compass does not tell you where to go. It tells you where north is, so that you can decide the next step yourself.

First principle

“Love and honor God in everything, offering every moment to His will and consecrating your entire life to Him: family, work, money, body, time and rest; nothing is left out.”

These 32 principles are not aspirations or words of encouragement. They are the criteria I decide with: what I accept and what I refuse, what I hold on to when it costs and what I let go of when it gets in the way. They are written in the second person because that is how they read best — the way a man speaks to himself before acting.

If My Pathway answers where I am going, My Compass answers what I decide with. Goals change shape over the years; these principles are what should not change while the pathway is being corrected.

III

The 32 principles

In the order they were written, from the first to the last.

I
The Foundation

How I stand before God and before myself.

01 — 08
01

Consecration

Love and honor God in everything, offering every moment to His will and consecrating your entire life to Him: family, work, money, body, time and rest; nothing is left out.

02

Gratitude

Live with gratitude and humility, seeing everything as a gift from God, embracing life with holy detachment and always trusting in His providence.

03

Dignity

Know yourself as a creature loved by God. Know you are loved unconditionally and love from that Love: what you do is born of who you are in Christ, and what you have is a means to serve better.

04

Attunement

Recognize the movements of God in prayer, with humility and in the light of the Gospel and the Church; let the Holy Spirit guide your step and live a daily “yes” of docility and obedience.

05

Illumination

Open your mind and heart to divine inspiration; recognize that God can use your creativity to open paths of goodness, truth and beauty in service of the Gospel and of your neighbor.

06

Wisdom

Seek divine wisdom in the Word, in prayer and in the sacraments; release what clouds your vision and build your path toward God in Christ upon that sacred nourishment.

07

Honor

Honor your word and conduct yourself with dignity and justice before God and men, treating your neighbor as you wish to be treated, according to the command of Christ.

08

Joy

Keep the wonder and trust of a child, and seek memorable experiences with your family and friends, recognizing God as the source of all true joy.

II
Conduct

How I work, decide and relate to others.

09 — 16
09

Balance

Seek equilibrium and harmony through moderation and healthy limits. Saying “no” to what pulls you away from God opens space to heal and to say “yes” to what strengthens your vocation.

10

Growth

Always use reason and seek to grow in virtue and holiness, without fear of failing, knowing that with God's grace every challenge well lived forms you in wisdom, humility and love.

11

Intelligence

Adapt with interior freedom, give yourself over to the truth, align your decisions with your vocation and turn uncertainty into growth and redemptive meaning.

12

Cooperation

Listen, be generous and of service; cooperate with humility, quieting the ego and placing God at the center. As part of the Body of Christ, use your gifts to serve your neighbor.

13

Proactivity

Be proactive and responsible: do what you can and align your mind and your efforts with what God places in your heart and with the opportunities for grace and service He offers you.

14

Effectiveness

Establish routines and good habits; be consistent, plan and prioritize, managing everything in service of your vocation and of God's will in your life.

15

Dedication

Have strength, courage and discipline; work with dedication and give your best, offering your effort to God, enjoying the process and without attachment to the outcome, trusting in His will.

16

Vocation

Make your life and your talents a response to God's call, directing them to serve others and to build the world according to the Gospel, for the glory of God and the good of your neighbor.

III
Life in Christ

Where everything above is fed and sustained.

17 — 22
17

Communion

Love God above all things, seek His communion in prayer and the sacraments, and be His instrument wherever He places you, aligning thought, word and deed with His will.

18

Holiness

Live your call to holiness, growing more like Christ each day. Grow in virtue and charity, letting grace transform your mind and your works, faithful in small things.

19

Redemption

Recognize that every good comes from God. Consecrate your life so that Christ may purify what is not His, raise up what is good and return it to its origin: God Himself.

20

Grace

Live and remain in a state of grace, with a full sacramental life. Avoid mortal sin and return quickly to grace. If you fall, drift away or lose your way, always return to God.

21

Marian Devotion

Live as a son in the school of Mary: consecrate yourself to her guidance, learn her humility and give your trusting yes to God, letting yourself be led to Christ.

22

Ecclesiality

Live your faith in communion with the Church, form your conscience in her Magisterium and serve with active humility within the Body of Christ.

IV
Abandonment

What to do with what cannot be controlled.

23 — 28
23

Faith

Let God's providence work; trust that He guides your story. Embrace every ending as a new beginning and say: “Jesus, I trust in You,” even without understanding the way.

24

Abandonment

Give yourself over to God's will, offering your plans and fears to His providence and trusting in His care. Accept what you do not control and say: “Jesus, I abandon myself to You.”

25

Discernment

Discern your interior life: in consolation, give thanks without making major decisions; in desolation, change nothing essential, limit your actions and stay faithful; in calm, build habits and move forward with reason.

26

Purity

Abstain from what darkens God's light in you; flee from sin and from all harm to your soul. Seek purification and repentance, letting yourself be transformed by His grace.

27

Oblation

Carry your cross with love: offer up suffering, choose healthy sacrifices and unite every trial to Christ, so that pain becomes offering and charity, not bitterness or self-pity.

28

Stewardship

Be a faithful guardian of the responsibilities and blessings God entrusts to you, remembering that you are a steward and not an owner. Use everything to serve, to love and to give glory to God.

V
The Destination

Where everything points in the end.

29 — 32
29

Eternity

Live oriented toward the final end: remember your eternal destiny, order your decisions to the judgment of God and act in the light of heaven, not of temporal success.

30

Governance

Exercise with humility the authority God entrusts to you to lead others in His name, without ego, without attachment, discerning, correcting and justly accepting the consequences.

31

Incarnation

Embody the values of the Gospel in your actions, turning faith into concrete works in service of your neighbor and the glory of God, with fidelity and trust in Providence.

32

Mercy

Make God's love visible by serving the vulnerable generously, seeing Christ in every face and placing charity above your own comfort.

IV

Pathway and compass

The two documents are meant to be read together: one says where, the other says with what.

“Make your life and your talents a response to God's call.”
