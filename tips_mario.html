I

An important note

Before downloading the document, please read the following.

Personal opinion · Not an institutional position
This does not represent the position of Tec de Monterrey

These 17 tips are not part of the course, they are not an extension of the syllabus and they do not claim to represent the official position of Tecnológico de Monterrey on any of the subjects addressed here. They are simply personal reflections that I have learned, thought about and come to value over the course of my own life — in a strictly personal capacity, as a teacher and not as an institutional spokesperson.

Notice about tip 17
Tip 17 is not secular — it is religious

You already know the first 16 tips, the ones we went through in class. The full document adds a 17th tip, and it is important that you know, before downloading it, that this final one is not secular: it is religious in nature and places God at the center of the reflection.

If you are an atheist, or if you feel that a tip of this nature might make you uncomfortable or offend you, I would rather recommend that you not download the full document, or that you stop before reaching that final section.

That said, it is worth mentioning that the tip is compatible with Judeo-Christian theology and, to a certain extent, with Muslim theology as well — it is not a sectarian position, nor one that excludes any particular tradition, but a personal reflection on faith, Providence and purpose.
