The Transcendental Dimension

Not a vertical. Not introduced or proposed — enabled only when the client permits it. TMG does not convert, preach, or evangelize: it orders pre-existing spiritual material.

Four signals of interior openness
Crisis of meaningemptiness or loss of direction despite material success.
Control fatigueexhaustion from the constant need to manage uncertainty.
Non-superficial moral languageresponsibility, purpose, and legacy appear as genuine inquiry.
Discomfort with one's own incoherencethe gap between what one believes and how one lives.
Four intervention routes — by level of integration
I Reconstruction — reestablish a minimum base of order in one's being.
II Alignment — close the gap between faith and operational life.
III Integration — full coherence between spirit, decision, and material construction.
IV Direction & Mission — clarify and execute a mission of greater impact.
Entry condition: the client must identify as Catholic, whether explicitly or implicitly — practicing, cultural, distant, or non-integrated profiles all qualify. The quality of their practice is not the criterion; the existence of a prior identity is. In the absence of this condition, the transcendental dimension is not activated under any circumstance — and in every case, activation requires the client's explicit consent.
