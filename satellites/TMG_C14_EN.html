Structure stabilizes

The design that determines whether the system works once reality stops being favorable. A company without structure collapses under pressure. One with structure can capitalize on it.

Purpose directs

The point from which decisions are made when information is incomplete — which is always. It isn't decorative. It's the decision mechanism when financial logic alone isn't enough.

Adaptation sustains

The system's capacity to learn from disruption and reconfigure without losing coherence. The difference between robust and antifragile. One withstands the blow. The other improves because of it.

Enabling relationships

  • Key Resources (agentic AI) → Key Activities — enables continuous stress-testing
  • Social Benefits → Customer Relationships — reinforces trust and reputation
  • Key Partners (community of principals) → Channels — reinforces quality referrals
  • Commitment to Talent → Value Proposition — sustains the promise of continuity without rotation

Strategic tensions

  • Higher Purpose ↔ Revenue Streams — radical exclusivity vs. business growth
  • Talent ↔ Revenue Streams — a single operator vs. scaling revenue
  • Social Costs ↔ Customer Segments — exclusivity vs. accessibility and broad impact
  • Cost Structure ↔ Talent — Mario's time, a scarce resource, vs. growing demand
